## News Aggregation

Comprehensive AI-powered news aggregation and analysis tools.

- **[ai-news-radar](https://github.com/LearnPrompt/ai-news-radar)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ - Real-time AI news tracking with 10+ web sources, OPML RSS support, 24h dual-view (AI-focused/All), and automatic failed source handling. *Most Active*
- **[clawfeed](https://github.com/kevinho/clawfeed)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ - Multi-frequency digest system (4h/daily/weekly/monthly) with AI deep analysis, bookmark features, and multi-user support via Google OAuth. *Most Complete*
- **[TrendRadar](https://github.com/sansan0/TrendRadar)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ - Enterprise-grade information push with MCP AI analysis, 10+ push channels (WeChat, Telegram, DingTalk, Feishu, Email, etc.), and unified timeline scheduling. *Enterprise Ready*
- **[DAK News](https://github.com/LittleLittleCloud/The-Grand-Archive)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ - News archive designed for AI, covering 30+ sources and updating every 30 minutes, with full-text search, REST API, SDK, and CLI access. *Production API*
- **[auto-news](https://github.com/finaldie/auto-news)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ - Automatic news aggregator with LLM for catching trends in minutes.
- **[hot_news_daily_push](https://github.com/tuber0613/hot_news_daily_push)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ - Multi-platform hot news collection with RSS, Twitter feeds, deduplication, and multi-channel push.
- **[meridian](https://github.com/iliane5/meridian)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ - Scrapes hundreds of sources, analyzes with AI, delivers personalized daily briefs.
- **[ai-daily-digest](https://github.com/vigorX777/ai-daily-digest)** ⭐⭐⭐ - TypeScript + Bun powered five-step pipeline for daily AI digests.
- **[ai-daily-skill](https://github.com/geekjourneyx/ai-daily-skill)** ⭐⭐⭐ - Daily AI skill reports and learning resources.
- **[CloudFlare-AI-Insight-Daily](https://github.com/justlovemaki/CloudFlare-AI-Insight-Daily)** ⭐⭐⭐ - Cloudflare Workers + Gemini powered daily AI insights with GitHub Pages deployment.

## RSS Management & Enhancement

Tools for RSS feed processing, filtering, and AI enhancement.

- **[FeedCraft](https://github.com/Colin-XKL/FeedCraft)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ - High-performance Go-based RSS generator (HTML/Curl/Search to RSS) with AI integration and custom prompts. *Best Architecture*
- **[RSS-Master](https://github.com/TD21forever/RSS-Master)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ - Custom filtering, AI summarization, scoring, and async processing for RSS feeds.
- **[RSSBox](https://github.com/versun/RSSBox)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ - Intelligent RSS management for efficient information retrieval (formerly RSS Translator).
- **[FreshRSS](https://github.com/FreshRSS/FreshRSS)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ - Mature multi-user RSS aggregator with 20+ languages, plugin system, and WebSub support. *Production Ready*
- **[FeedMe](https://github.com/Seanium/FeedMe)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ - Lightweight React-based RSS reader with AI summaries and zero-cost deployment.
- **[AI-RSS](https://github.com/wolfitem/AI-RSS)** ⭐⭐⭐ - AI-enhanced RSS reading experience.
- **[huntly](https://github.com/lcomplete/huntly)** ⭐⭐⭐ - Information management and knowledge organization tool.

## Podcast Generation

Convert RSS feeds and news into AI-generated podcasts.

- **[PodRSS](https://github.com/LetTTGACO/PodRSS)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ - Next.js + Cloudflare Workers powered RSS to podcast with AI summaries and Edge TTS synthesis.
- **[rss_ai_podcast](https://github.com/astralwaveio/rss_ai_podcast)** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ - Multi-source RSS with Gemini summaries, Azure TTS, and Telegram/WeChat push.
- **[rss2podcast](https://github.com/intothevoid/rss2podcast)** ⭐⭐⭐ - Locally hosted AI-generated podcast from RSS feeds.
- **[newpodcastforAI](https://github.com/zacfire/newpodcastforAI)** ⭐⭐⭐ - Curated AI-related podcast RSS aggregation.
